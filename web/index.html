FM1-Chord

A chord player firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1: your own chord on every key, a synth made for chords, MIDI out, strum, arpeggio, latch and voice leading. With an editor that suggests what to play next.

EditorBuild chord sets and hear them here, with harmony suggestions, progressions and MIDI export. Works without the FM-1 (and in Safari on iPad). Try the demoThe editor with a simulated FM-1. Firmware flasherInstall FM1-Chord on the FM-1 from Chrome or Edge.

Beta, not yet tried on a real FM-1. Read the recovery guide before you install anything. Source: github.com/TwistedGenetics/FM1-Chord-. Free software, GPL-3.0. Not affiliated with M-VAVE.
